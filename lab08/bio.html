Kate Batista's Biography

About Me

Hi, I am Katharine (Kate) Batista! I am a senior at Syracuse University majoring in Applied Data Science with a concentration in Web Development . I have been in the Renee Crown University Honors program here at SU, maintaining honor roll status consecutively over the last two years. I love all things UI/UX and data engineering. I spent my second summer working with Blue Raven, an aerospace engineering materials company. During that time, I have completed two internships with them. One in Information technology Services and one in Data Science. These experiences have been amazing and nothing short of fruitful when it comes to workplace experience and technical development.

Personal Information

Outside of my work with the iSchool. I am passionate about yoga, food, and music. When I am not glued to a computer, you can find me taking long walks, trying new recipes with my roommate and doing yoga in my living room. I love to craft with friends and explore creative avenues I may not have time for during the school year. Holidays are my favorite time of the year, and I believe birthdays are beyond special for every person. I grew up in suburban New Jersey with 3 older brothers and 2 dogs. My house was always lively for much of my childhood. I am an only daughter, which sometimes feels like being an only child. I spent a lot of time entertaining myself and developing a close relationship with my pets. I have two dogs; their names are Juniper ad Po. They are the heart and soul of my life.

As I enter my final year at Syracuse University, I am shocked to be a senior but really excited to see what fun, educational life experiences Syracuse, New York has in store for me. Post-grad, I would love to start my career as an data analyst.
